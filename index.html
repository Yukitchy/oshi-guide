How it works

Three steps. No account, no OshiGuide fee.

1

Tell us your fandom

The series you love, the city you're heading to, and your dates. That's the whole form.

2

Get matched with a local fan

We hand-match you with a local otaku guide who actually knows and loves your series — not a script-reader.

3

Book on the guide's own page

You book directly through the guide's existing listing on Viator, GetYourGuide and similar platforms, with their buyer protection.

OshiGuide never touches your payment. Matching is free — we just connect fans.
